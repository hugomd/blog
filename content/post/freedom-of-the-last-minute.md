---
title: "Freedom of the Last Minute"
date: 2024-07-08T22:21:22+01:00
tags: ["philosophical", "travel"]
description: "Justifying leaving things until the last minute."
---

Leaving logistical plans until the last minute leaves you with fewer decisions
to make, fewer options to compare, and more importantly, more time to enjoy
yourself.

If you accept that the future is in flux, and things won't always your way,
you'll end up stressing less, and going with the flow.

All this to say: _don't feel bad about booking international flights and trains
the day before_. What it might cost you in coin, you may gain in 
{{< sidenote "time, freedom, and new experiences!" 1 >}}Some of my fondest memories traveling
have been those made unexpectedly getting lost in a foreign city, or missing a
connection.{{< /sidenote >}}
