I didn't write a single post on this blog in 2023. I don't feel bad about it.

A summary of the year:
* I helped plan a wedding with my {{< sidenote "fiancée." 1 >}}She's now my wife.{{< /sidenote >}}
* I invested more time in exercise, mostly {{<sidenote "rock climbing" 1>}}I did a *lot* of rock climbing, and I'm still loving it.{{< /sidenote >}} and yoga.
* I learned more about photography.
* I learned to surf.
* I worked hard at my day job, and subsequently feel like I've accomplished.
  a lot, not only in terms of output, but also personal growth.
* I travelled, visiting family, holidaying, and for work.

That may seem like a lot, or not a lot, but to me it has certainly felt like
the former. I haven't had much time to write blog posts, and I'm wary of
creative burn out.
